# Mailkit design system

## Provenance

Extracted from the user’s Zermo Brands crest (gold T-rex pair, burgundy field) on 2026-08-22. The **app mark** replaces the ZB shield with a sealed gold envelope; the dinosaurs stay. Tokens are estimated from the brand images. Fonts are role-based from the Hallmark canon.

## System

- **Genre:** atmospheric
- **Macrostructure (app):** Workbench
- **Nav:** N3 side-rail (accounts)
- **Footer:** Ft2 status line
- **Tone:** luxury infrastructure
- **Motion:** fade only; no hover-scale; `prefers-reduced-motion` honored

## Tokens

See `tokens.css`. Accent is gold, used as a highlighter (active rail, unread dot, primary border), not a flood fill. Paper is night-burgundy, not `#000`.

## Notes — do not carry over

- Italic display headers (the marketing banners use italic for “actually needs”; app headings stay roman).
- Fake browser chrome around the workbench.
- Invented metrics or testimonial counts.
- Guessing custom-domain IMAP hosts in the UI.

## App mark

`desktop/brand/icon-1024.png` and `desktop/brand/AppIcon.icns` — two engraved T-rexes flanking a gold envelope with a burgundy wax seal, burgundy field.
